# Human Review Checklist (QA gate)
**Purpose:** EVERY client-facing deliverable passes this gate before delivery. Named human signs. This is the liability firewall that makes the AI-leveraged model insurable.

## Gate: for every deliverable (report, policy, questionnaire response, board deck, evidence pack)
- [ ] **Q1 — A named human prepared/reviewed this** (name on the document, not "AI")
- [ ] **Q2 — No fabricated evidence:** every fact, date, screenshot, or control claim traces to a real source in the client's workspace
- [ ] **Q3 — Audit-safe language:** no "certified," "authorized," "compliant" claims about the client; no certification-authority overreach (we advise; 3PAOs/CPAs certify)
- [ ] **Q4 — Plain-English check:** a board member of a 20-person nonprofit understands the executive summary
- [ ] **Q5 — Award-linked:** every risk statement ties to a named funder/contract or is removed
- [ ] **Q6 — No CUI/PII leakage:** no sensitive data in drafts, prompts, or shared docs beyond the client workspace
- [ ] **Q7 — Grant-memo attached** where money changed hands (references/grant-justification-memo.md)
- [ ] **Q8 — Date + version:** doc versioned, dated, stored in client vault (not just our drive)

## Sign-off line (all deliverables)
> Reviewed and approved by: <Name, credential> — <date>
> [Agency] provides advisory services only and does not act as a 3PAO, CPA firm, or certification body.

## Post-gate audits
- **Weekly:** sample 2 delivered items per consultant vs. this checklist (founder does it first 3 months).
- **Per incident/audit-failure:** root-cause in 48h, update the relevant template/prompt, add the lesson to this checklist if it's a gate gap.

## What fails the gate
- Unverifiable claims, missing sign-off, boilerplate not tailored to the client, any "we guarantee certification" language (→ refer out, do not soften).
