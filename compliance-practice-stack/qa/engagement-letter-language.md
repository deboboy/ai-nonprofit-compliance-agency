# Engagement Letter Language (liability core)
**Purpose:** Boilerplate that ships in every engagement letter. Paste into the letter; keep the sections in order. Human legal review before first use.

## Core clauses (boilerplate)

**1. Scope & advisory nature.** [Agency] provides advisory, readiness, and preparation services related to information security compliance ("Services"). [Agency] does not act as a Third-Party Assessment Organization (3PAO), CPA firm, certification body, or attorney. Services do not constitute certification, attestation, or an audit opinion. Certification and attestation authority rests exclusively with licensed assessors and CPA firms engaged separately by Client.

**2. No guarantee.** [Agency] does not guarantee that Client will achieve or maintain any certification, authorization, or favorable audit outcome. Services are designed to improve readiness and reduce risk; outcomes depend on Client's implementation, personnel, and third-party assessors.

**3. Client obligations.** Client is responsible for implementing remediation items, maintaining evidence, and providing accurate and complete information. [Agency] relies on information Client provides; [Agency] is not liable for consequences of Client's inaccurate or incomplete information.

**4. Evidence.** [Agency] will not fabricate, backdate, or misrepresent evidence. Where evidence does not exist, deliverables will identify gaps. Client agrees not to request or rely upon fabricated evidence.

**5. Data handling.** Client data, including any Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) or personal data, remains Client's property. [Agency] processes it only to perform Services, in accordance with the DPA (attached), and stores it in Client's designated workspace where feasible. [Agency]'s AI tooling does not receive raw CUI/PII beyond the minimum necessary for a given task.

**6. Insurance.** [Agency] maintains professional liability (E&O) and cyber liability insurance with limits of at least <$1M/$2M> and will provide certificates upon request.

**7. Fees & grant mapping.** Fees are as stated in the SOW. [Agency] will provide grant-justification memos for invoices at Client's request, but Client is solely responsible for the allowability of expenditures under its awards.

**8. Term & termination.** Annual term, auto-renewing unless either party gives 60 days' notice. Fees paid are non-refundable except for unused prepaid months.

**9. Limitation of liability.** To the maximum extent permitted by law, [Agency]'s aggregate liability arising from Services is limited to fees paid by Client in the 12 months preceding the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

**10. Governing law & disputes.** <State> law; binding arbitration for disputes under $50,000.

## Marketing guardrails (bind the whole org)
- Never use "FedRAMP compliant," "GovRAMP authorized," or "certified" in reference to [Agency] or its clients.
- Case studies name the frameworks *prepared against* ("readiness for NIST 800-171") not achieved status.
- All public claims require the founder's sign-off (same gate as deliverables).
