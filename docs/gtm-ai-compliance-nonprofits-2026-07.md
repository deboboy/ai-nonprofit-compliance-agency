**Builds on:** `dd-ai-compliance-agency-nonprofits-2026-07.md` (due diligence, July 2026)
**Prepared:** July 2026 · Pricing/market facts verified as of Aug 2026 sources
**Position:** The practice that makes the $500–$2,500/mo price point work — where Vanta/Drata won't go and vCISO firms can't scale.

---

## 0. Thesis restated

Platform incumbents (Vanta, Drata, Secureframe) effectively start at **~$7,500/yr** and hit **$20K–$25K median** — with no grant-compliance context, no funder relationships, and no nonprofit verticalization. Nonprofit-specialist vCISO firms exist but are tiny, generalist, human-heavy, and non-AI. The gap: a **services-led, AI-leveraged compliance office** priced at $500–$2,500/mo that nonprofits can pay out of grant funds, with human sign-off and auditor credibility.

This plan covers: positioning → segments → offer/pricing → channels → sales motion → 90-day launch → metrics → economics.

---

## 1. Positioning

**One-liner:** *"We keep your grant-funded programs compliant so you keep the grant."*

**Positioning statement:** For executive directors and COOs of small nonprofits (budget $1M–$20M) holding federal, state, or foundation funding, [Agency] is the AI-leveraged compliance office that translates security mandates (NIST 800-171 flow-down, GSA CUI rules, GovRAMP vendor requirements, SOC 2) into plain-English, funder-ready compliance — at one-fifth the cost of traditional consulting and with a human who signs their name.

**Why us vs. the alternatives (competitive wedge):**

| Alternative | Why it fails for this buyer | Our answer |
|---|---|---|
| Vanta/Drata/Secureframe ($7.5K–$25K/yr) | Built for VC SaaS; framework-mismatched; no grant context; no hand-holding; too expensive | We sell *outcomes for funders*, not software licenses |
| Generalist vCISO/MSP ($2K–$10K/mo) | Don't speak nonprofit; no grant flow-down expertise; no funder channels | Vertical focus + funder-fluency + grant-priced packages |
| Big 4 / FedRAMP advisory ($100K+) | Wrong customer, wrong economics | We don't sell authorization — we sell navigation |
| DIY (CISA, TechSoup, templates) | No staff hours to execute; docs go stale | We do the work; AI makes us cheap enough to |

**Positioning guardrails (from diligence):**
- Never claim to *certify*. We advise/prepare; 3PAOs/CPAs certify. Marketing language must be audit-safe.
- Lead with **grant protection**, not "cybersecurity" — the board/ED purchase trigger is funding continuity.
- Never position FedRAMP/GovRAMP *authorization* as the service. Position **flow-down navigation and vendor verification**.

## 3. Offer architecture & pricing

### 3.1 The wedge: Compliance Readiness Assessment ($3,500–$6,500, one-time)
Deliverable: 20-page plain-English report — (a) which frameworks *actually* apply to their funder mix, (b) gap analysis vs. NIST 800-171/53 & SOC 2 trust criteria, (c) prioritized 90-day remediation plan, (d) **funder-questionnaire pack** (answers to the 5 most common grant security questions), (e) vendor GovRAMP/FedRAMP verification for their top 10 vendors. AI accelerates intake + control mapping + evidence inventory; human validates and signs.

### 3.2 The retainer: Compliance Office (tiers)
| Tier | Price/mo | What's included | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Standards** | $500/mo | Policy library management, monthly evidence collection (AI-driven), funder-questionnaire responses, quarterly posture report, 1 support hour/mo | $1M–$3M orgs, 1 framework |
| **Plus** | $1,250/mo | Everything above + 8 support hrs/mo, SSP/POA&M maintenance, vendor verification cadence, audit-preparation runs, board-ready quarterly deck | $3M–$10M orgs, 1–2 frameworks |
| **Fractional Security Office** | $2,500/mo | Everything above + fractional vCISO (quarterly strategy, incident response on-call, board/ED briefings), 20 hrs/mo, multi-framework (e.g., 800-171 + SOC 2) | $10M–$20M orgs, 2+ frameworks |

**Packaging rules:**
- Annual contracts (12-mo, paid quarterly) — churn protection; offer 2 months free on annual prepay.
- **Grant-billable by design:** every invoice line maps to a grant-allowable cost category (tech/security is an allowed NSGP, SLCGP, and many foundation grant expense). Provide a "grant justification memo" per deliverable — this is a differentiator no platform offers.
- Project fees: SOC 2 Readiness Sprint $10K–$18K; CMMC Gap Assessment $8K–$15K (partnered); GovRAMP vendor-verification service $1,500/quarter per vendor portfolio.
- **Foundation-pass-through pricing:** foundations can buy N months of service for N grantees (portfolio deals at 20% discount).

### 3.3 Why the price point is defensible
- AI tooling stack (document gen, control mapping, evidence aggregation, questionnaire drafting) runs <$15K/yr — fixed cost shared across all clients.
- Delivery leverage: 1 senior consultant + agents serves 20–40 retainers vs. 5–8 for a traditional firm.
- Incumbents can't match price *with* vertical depth — their model is software licenses + generalist services, not grant-fluent delivery.

**Assets (90 days):**

**Distribution:** LinkedIn (ED/COO/CFO of nonprofits; association groups), nonprofit association newsletters (via partners), NTEN/TechSoup communities, grant-writer forums, quarterly "Grant Security Briefing" webinar (newsletter list).

## 6. Sales motion

**The funnel:**
1. **Inbound:** Scorecard + gated guides → discovery call (15-min, qualification)
2. **Qualification (20-min):** confirm ≥$1M budget, ≥2 gov grants, real funding-at-risk, authority to buy
3. **Paid diagnostic: $500 "Funded-Security Review"** — 30-min call + 2-page finding → converts to full assessment. (Small fee filters tire-kickers and offsets discovery cost.)
4. **Assessment pitch → close** ($3.5K–$6.5K, then retainer within 60–90 days)
5. **Land & expand:** Standards → Plus → Fractional Security Office; grant cycle renewal = natural upsell moment (they got the award, now protect it)

**Sales targets:** 25 discovery calls/mo → 10 diagnostics → 4 assessments → 2 retainers/mo at steady state. Founder closes first 20 deals; hire closer at ~15 retainers.

**Objection handling:**
- "No budget" → "This maps to your NSGP/SLCGP/foundation TA budget — we include the grant justification memo." + CISA State/Local Cybersecurity Grant Program ($1B program; nonprofits often eligible) & FEMA NSGP ($150K max).
- "We're too small to be a target" → "Funders don't agree — your GSA contract says otherwise."
- "Isn't this Vanta's job?" → "Vanta starts at $7,500/yr and doesn't know your funder's language. We're less than that *with* a human who signs."

## 7. 90-day launch roadmap

**Days 1–30 — Prove the wedge:**
- Build Scorecard + Decoder (gated assets); stand up AI delivery stack (document gen, control mapping, evidence capture, questionnaire engine).
- Close **3 paid assessments** from warm network (foundations, fiscal sponsors, ED contacts).
- Sign 2 state nonprofit associations to soft-partner pilots.

**Days 31–60 — Land retainers:**
- Convert the 3 assessments → 3 Standards/Plus retainers.
- First webinar ("GSA CUI rules: what they mean for your grant") via association partner.
- Publish state GovRAMP lookup page (SEO seed).

**Days 61–90 — Systematize & prove economics:**
- 3 more assessments + 3 more retainers (target: 6 retainers, ~$6K MRR).
- Document the delivery playbook (templates, agent prompts, QA checklist) so the second hire is a template-follower, not a specialist.
- CMMC partner conversations for the Nov 2026 surge; NSGP-season content push (NSGP applications typically open fall).
- **Checkpoint:** CAC < $2K, assessment→retainer conversion ≥ 50%, gross margin ≥ 60%, zero audit failures.

## 9. Economics recap (from diligence, tightened)

- Reinvestment order: (1) closer/sales role, (2) delivery analyst, (3) content/SEO specialist.

## 10. Risks & mitigations (GTM-specific)

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Nonprofits still won't pay services | Grant-memo packaging + funder-paid portfolios; price anchors at "one missed grant = 10× your annual fee" |
| Platform incumbents add nonprofit pricing | Moat is channel (associations, fiscal sponsors, grant writers) + funder fluency, not software |
| CMMC surge collapses after Nov 2026 | It's a wedge, not the business — core is 800-171/SOC 2/GovRAMP flow-down, which is perpetual |
| AI output liability | Human-sign-off policy, E&O insurance, engagement letters disclaiming certification authority |
| Sales velocity too slow for bootstrap | Fee-based diagnostics ($500) keep funnel warm; partner-referral CAC near zero |
