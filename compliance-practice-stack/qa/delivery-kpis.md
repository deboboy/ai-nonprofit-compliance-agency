# Delivery KPIs
**Purpose:** The numbers that say the delivery machine is working. Review monthly by the founder.

## Per-consultant delivery metrics
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Retainers managed per consultant | 15–20 | CRM |
| Delivery hours per retainer/month | ≤ 4 | Time tracking |
| Assessment→retainer conversion | ≥ 50% | CRM funnel |
| On-time deliverable rate | ≥ 95% | QA log |
| Evidence pack completeness (monthly) | ≥ 90% of clients | Evidence index |
| Questionnaire SLA (1-day) | ≥ 90% within SLA | Ticket log |
| Client-reported audit/assessment pass | 100% on prepared scope | Post-audit survey |

## Quality & risk metrics
| Metric | Target | Note |
|---|---|---|
| QA gate failures (deliverables bounced) | < 5% | Founder samples weekly |
| Fabricated-evidence incidents | **0** | Non-negotiable; any → root-cause + process fix |
| E&O claims / audit-failure disputes | 0 | Escalate immediately |
| CUI/PII mishandling incidents | **0** | DPA breach protocol |

## Financial delivery metrics
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Gross margin (delivery) | ≥ 65% | Accounting |
| Grant-memo attach rate | 100% on invoiced retainers | Billing log |
| Revenue per consultant | ≥ $220K/yr | Accounting |

## North star (from GTM plan)
**Grant dollars protected** — sum of client award value covered by active retainers: **$50M+ by month 12**. Reported in every monthly review and used in every case study.
